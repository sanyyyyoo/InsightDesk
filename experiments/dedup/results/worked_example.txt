A sentence is filler if (a) near-copies (cosine >= 0.90) occur in >= 8 complaints AND
(b) those complaints are otherwise unrelated: cohesion of their remaining text <= 0.30, where
    cohesion = ( sum_i sum_j cos(rest_i, rest_j) - n ) / ( n (n - 1) )   (mean over the n(n-1) off-diagonal pairs)
